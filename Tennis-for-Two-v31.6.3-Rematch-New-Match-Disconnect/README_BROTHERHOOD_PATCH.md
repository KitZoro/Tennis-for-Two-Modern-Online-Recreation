# v31.2 — Sonic and Tails Brotherhood Story Patch

This patch keeps the v31.1 gameplay/networking foundation and adds a new 2D Story Mode chapter.

## Chapter 5 — BROTHERHOOD

A tribute to the fan project **Sonic and Tails Brotherhood**, especially Chapter 9.

- Edwin remains the playable grey bunny.
- Kit is drawn as a platinum fox with glowing blue eyes.
- Jim and Mick are correctly named.
- The chapter includes the Jim/Mick confrontation and a playable hard-CPU Tennis for Two challenge.
- The post-match dialogue contains the Chapter 9 reference and explicitly labels the chapter as a tribute.
- Jim, Mick, and the Guard use neutral placeholder portraits so the game does not invent canonical character designs for them.

## Controls

Story dialogue: ENTER / SPACE to continue, ESC to return.

Tennis challenge: the normal Player 1 controls from v31.1.

## Networking

`network.py`, `rollback.py`, and the v31 Fast Input Network behavior were not intentionally modified by this story patch.
