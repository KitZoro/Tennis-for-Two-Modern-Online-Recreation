CUSTOM MUSIC

Drop as many .ogg, .mp3, or .wav files here as you want.

Choose 9 - CHOOSE BGM from the main menu, use Up/Down to highlight a song,
and press Enter to play it. The choice is saved for the next launch. Built-in
Synth is always available, even when this folder is empty.

Controls:
    M   mute/unmute music only
    N   mute/unmute sound effects only
    F5  reload this folder while the game is running
    R   rescan this folder from the BGM selection screen

If no compatible file is found, Tennis for Two uses its built-in synthesized
music instead.

For music you redistribute with the game, use music you created or have
permission/license to distribute.
